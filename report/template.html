Who moved the needle

Win Probability Added (WPA) credits each play's swing to the player who made it: made shots and free throws to the shooter, turnovers to the ball-handler, and a miss to the shooter once the defense secures the rebound. Summed over a season it rewards production that happened when games were still in doubt.

How it works

  • Data. stats.nba.com play-by-play for 2019-20 to 2025-26 (8,286 games; 2025-26 converted from the newer v3 feed), via the public shufinskiy/nba_data archive. Shot locations from the same source.
  • Game state. Score margin, time left, who has the ball (inferred from made shots, rebounds, turnovers and jump balls), overtime, and pre-game team strength.
  • Pre-game strength. An Elo rating replayed game by game in schedule order, so each game only sees results from before it (no leakage).
  • Models. Logistic regression with engineered features (e.g. margin ÷ √minutes left, prior strength fading as the clock runs) against monotonic gradient-boosted trees. Selected on 2024-25, refit, then scored once on 2025-26.
  • Limitations. No lineup or player-availability data, so a game a star sits out is priced like any other. WPA here is offensive and play-level; defensive stops are not credited to individual defenders.
